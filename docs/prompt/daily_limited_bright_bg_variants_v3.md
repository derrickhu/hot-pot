# Daily Limited – Bright Background Variants v3

目标：替代当前偏暗、视觉杂乱的 `bg_meadow_picnic_v2` / `bg_tropical_orchard_v2`。

风格基线（必须遵守）：

- **极高明度、低饱和度、清爽干净**，不要厚涂、不要浓郁色块。
- 整张画面像 watercolor / soft children illustration，颜色非常通透。
- **画面中央留出 60% 的"白噪/纯净"区域**（白卡牌区会盖在这里），不要在中央画大叶、大花朵、大水果等强对比物。
- 仅在画面 **上方 20% 与下方 25%** 做淡淡的氛围装饰（草地 / 小花 / 飘叶 / 远山 / 太阳光斑）。
- 装饰物使用极淡颜色（不饱和），透明感强，可加 ~15% 不透明度的 watercolor wash。
- **不要任何文字、不要 UI 元素、不要框、不要明显高对比物体**。
- **9:16 vertical**，1080×1920 输出（NB2 出 768×1376 即可）。

颜色基调（任选其一）：

- 浅蓝天 + 浅嫩绿草地（晨光感）
- 浅薄荷绿 + 奶油黄渐变（汽水感）
- 浅奶白 + 极淡薄荷绿 + 一丝樱花粉点缀

---

## 变体 1：`bg_bright_meadow_morning_v3`

```
9:16 vertical illustration, very bright and airy summer meadow morning,
1080x1920 output. Style: soft watercolor, super clean, low saturation,
high luminance, gentle children-book vibe, NOT dense, NOT photorealistic.

Composition (top to bottom):
- Top 20%: very pale sky blue gradient (#dff1ff -> #f6fcff), tiny soft white
  watercolor clouds with very low opacity, two or three faint flying birds
  silhouettes in pastel gray, optional faint sun light wash in the upper right
  corner.
- Middle 55%: NEARLY EMPTY clean off-white / very light cream wash (#fbf8ec).
  Just a few floating tiny flower petals or pollen dots far apart, all in
  super soft pastel (pale pink, pale yellow, pale mint), each at ~12-18%
  opacity. NO large objects, NO leaves, NO fruits, NO ribbons, no patterns.
- Bottom 25%: very light grass meadow strip in pale spring green (#cdeac0 ->
  #e8f5d4), with sparse soft grass tufts and tiny daisies (pale yellow center
  + white petals) and a couple of pale lavender wildflowers, all painted very
  loosely.

Hard rules:
- Background is overall extremely BRIGHT, never gray or muddy.
- No frames, no UI, no text, no logos, no watermark.
- The middle band (about y=420 to y=1500) MUST be visually almost empty so
  white game cards can sit on top with very high contrast.
- No dense foliage, no overlapping leaves, no shadows in the center.
- All decoration uses watercolor wash, transparent feel, NOT bold cartoon.
```

## 变体 2：`bg_bright_soda_breeze_v3`

```
9:16 vertical illustration, fresh summer soda breeze, very bright pastel
mood, 1080x1920 output. Style: clean watercolor + light flat shading,
extremely airy, NOT dense, NOT photorealistic.

Composition (top to bottom):
- Top 20%: pale mint-to-cream vertical gradient (#e8fbe8 -> #fff8e0), one or
  two faint slices of lemon and lime in the upper corners, drawn as super
  light line art with watercolor fill at ~25% opacity, plus a few pale
  bubble/sparkle shapes scattered around the very top.
- Middle 55%: NEARLY EMPTY clean cream wash (#fdf8e3). Only a handful of
  tiny floating bubbles (pale white circles with thin outline) and a few
  super faint pastel confetti dots (pale yellow, pale mint, pale pink),
  spaced far apart. NO large objects in the middle, NO ribbons, NO frames.
- Bottom 25%: pale aqua-mint band (#cef0e0 -> #e6f7ec), suggesting a soda
  surface or fresh grass; a few light bubbles rising, and tiny pastel
  flower silhouettes near the bottom edge, all in low saturation.

Hard rules:
- Overall feel: very bright, refreshing, slightly cool tone, NOT yellow-green
  muddy.
- The center 60% MUST stay almost empty so white cards sit on top crisply.
- No frame, no UI, no text, no character, no fruit clusters in the middle.
- Decoration must look transparent / watercolor wash style, not solid cartoon.
```
