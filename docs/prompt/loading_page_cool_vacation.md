# Loading 页背景：清凉度假海边躺椅

## 用途

游戏启动 / 资源加载阶段的竖版全屏背景。画面要有清凉、舒适、度假感，和当前游戏内 2D 手绘漫画萌感一致，不写实、不 3D。

## Gemini 提示词

```text
Vertical mobile game loading screen background, 9:16 portrait composition, 2D hand-painted cartoon style, cute and cozy, same art family as a casual tropical fruit game, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D render, NOT anime character scene.

Scene mood: refreshing summer vacation, cool ocean breeze, relaxing and comfortable.

Far background: a calm blue sea facing the viewer, soft turquoise water, gentle waves, pale sky, a few fluffy white clouds, distant small island silhouettes, bright but not harsh sunlight. The horizon is high enough to show the sea view clearly.

Mid / foreground: a cute tropical beach terrace or resort deck with warm bamboo / light wood details, consistent with the game's bamboo hut UI theme. One cozy beach lounge chair in the foreground, angled slightly toward the sea, with soft mint / cream cushions and a small folded towel. The chair should feel inviting and cool.

Next to the lounge chair: a small round side table made of bamboo or light wood. On the table are many chilled fruits and pretty summer drinks: sliced watermelon, citrus slices, grapes, kiwi, strawberries, ice cubes, condensation drops, a glass of iced fruit tea, a colorful smoothie, a coconut drink with straw, mint leaves. Make the fruits look delicious and cute, with clean outlines and soft cel shading.

Overall palette: cool turquoise, mint green, soft cream, light bamboo brown, fresh fruit colors. Keep the image airy and bright. Use soft highlights, gentle sparkles, and light breeze feeling. No clutter.

Composition requirement: leave a clean readable space in the lower-middle area for game UI loading text / progress bar overlay, but do NOT draw any text or progress bar in the image. Keep important objects away from the top safe area and bottom home indicator area.

NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no logo, no watermark, no UI buttons, no people, no realistic photo texture, no heavy shadows.
```
