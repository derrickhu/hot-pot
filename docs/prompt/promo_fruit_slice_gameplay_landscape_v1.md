# 果切无尽玩法横版宣传图 v1

Use the provided gameplay screenshot as the reference for the real game layout and art style.

Create a 16:9 horizontal promotional illustration for a WeChat Mini Game fruit slicing / fruit matching mode.

## Art Direction

- Cute polished 2D casual mobile game advertising key art.
- Match the screenshot style: bright sky-blue background, soft clouds, juicy cartoon fruits, warm wooden boards, clean thick outlines.
- Recompose the screenshot into an attractive promotional key visual, not a raw UI screenshot.
- No phone frame, no black bars, no native WeChat menu, no direct screenshot crop.

## Composition

- Main subject: a lively field of large colorful fruits floating and falling through the sky.
- Bottom foreground: two slanted wooden boards forming a V-shaped funnel, with a small pipe/knife mechanism at the center bottom.
- Show gameplay action clearly: fruits are about to drop into the center pipe, a sliced fruit burst, golden coin reward sparkle, and a few motion streaks.
- Keep the center-right area readable and not too cluttered.
- Use strong silhouettes and large fruits so it reads well at thumbnail size.

Do not add too many different fruit types.

## Hard Bans

- NO text, NO Chinese characters, NO English letters, NO numbers.

- NO photorealism, NO hard 3D render, NO cluttered collage.

## Output

- 16:9 landscape source image.
- Leave enough safe space around the edges for later center-crop and resize to 1280x720.
