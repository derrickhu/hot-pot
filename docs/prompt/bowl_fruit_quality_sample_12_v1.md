# Bowl Fruit Quality Sample 12 v1

Generate a single 4 columns x 3 rows sprite sheet of high-quality hand-painted fruit bowl ingredients for a casual WeChat mini game.

Style target:
- 2D hand-painted cartoon food, top-down with slight 3/4 perspective.
- Thick warm dark-brown outer outline, clean readable silhouette.
- Juicy surface, soft watercolor texture, bright saturated fruit colors.
- Stronger material quality than flat stickers: visible top highlight, subtle edge shadow, tiny pulp / seed details, and a small contact shadow baked under each piece.
- Each ingredient should look like it is floating on a glossy dessert soup surface, but the background must remain easy to remove.
- Match the current game scale: small bowl toppings, not whole large fruits.

Canvas:
- 4 columns x 3 rows, each cell contains exactly one isolated ingredient.
- White or very light pastel solid background.
- Leave generous empty padding around every ingredient.
- NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no writing anywhere in the image.
- No bowl, no UI, no plate, no hands, no character.

Cells, left to right, top to bottom:
1. strawberry slice, red flesh, pale core, seeds, glossy highlight.
2. orange slice, round citrus wheel, translucent pulp segments.
3. kiwi slice, green flesh, black seeds, creamy center.
4. banana coin slice, pale yellow, soft brown rim.
5. purple grape cluster, 3-4 small round grapes, strong glossy highlights.
6. blueberry pair, deep blue-purple, white bloom, tiny stem mark.
7. pear wedge, pale cream flesh, red peel edge, one small seed.
8. pineapple ring, golden yellow, central hole, fibrous radial texture.
9. lemon slice, bright yellow citrus wheel, clear segment lines.
10. mango cube, golden orange cube, soft rounded corners.
11. coconut jelly cube, translucent milky white, soft blue shadow edge.
12. taro ball, lavender-purple chewy sphere, subtle powder texture.

Strict constraints:
- Keep all 12 cells visually consistent in line weight, light direction, saturation, and scale.
- Ingredients must not touch cell borders.
- Do not add random extra fruit pieces.
- Do not add fake text or symbols.
- Transparent-looking food edges are okay, but background should stay simple for rembg.
