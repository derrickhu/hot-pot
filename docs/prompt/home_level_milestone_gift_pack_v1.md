## Prompt（英文）

Single game UI gift box icon, 1:1 square canvas.

Style: cute casual mobile puzzle game, bright summer fruit theme, soft 2D cartoon illustration with gentle cel shading, NOT photoreal, NOT 3D render.

Subject: one large festive reward gift box, slightly tilted 15 degrees for energy. Box body vivid coral-red (#FF3B4F) with glossy highlight; lid slightly open showing golden glow inside. Bold golden-yellow ribbon (#FFD95A) tied in a big bow on top. Small sparkle stars and tiny fruit confetti (strawberry, lemon slice) around the box — subtle, not cluttered.

Colors must POP against a green UI button: high saturation red + gold + tiny white specular highlights. Add soft warm outer glow halo (yellow-orange) around the whole gift, but keep background fully transparent (no backdrop, no floor shadow on ground plane).

Composition: gift centered, fills ~75% of frame, leave safe margin for circular pulse glow in game.

Constraints:
- NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO Chinese characters, NO watermark, NO logo, NO UI frame.
- Transparent background only (checkerboard invisible in final PNG).
- Single object, clean silhouette for small display ~96px on screen.
