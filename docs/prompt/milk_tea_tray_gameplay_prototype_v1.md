# Milk Tea Tray Gameplay Prototype v1

Create a vertical mobile game UI concept art / gameplay prototype image for a cute casual WeChat mini game.

Use the attached reference screenshot only for gameplay structure and object camera angle, NOT for copying characters, UI icons, text, or exact style.

Art direction:
- Match our game's warm cute food-game style: soft cream and honey colors, rounded UI, hand-painted 2D cartoon, clean mobile game readability, cozy fruit tea / dessert theme.
- Bright but gentle palette: cream beige tabletop, peach-orange counter, mint green accents, translucent colorful fruit tea cups.
- No realistic rendering, no hard 3D, no dark cyber style.
- Object-focused gameplay view. The drinks, trays, board cells, and orders must be the visual focus.
- The top shopkeeper / customer area should be weakened: small, soft, blurred or simplified background silhouettes behind the counter, low detail, not stealing attention.

Composition, vertical 9:16 mobile screen:

1. Top background area, about 18 percent of screen:
   - A soft fruit tea shop counter background.
   - Faint simplified shopkeeper and customer silhouettes only, very low detail.
   - Warm window / counter shapes, cute but understated.

2. Top order strip, about 12 percent of screen:
   - A rounded peach-red order bar.
   - Five drink order icons displayed as individual fruit tea cups.
   - Each order is represented by one drink type only.
   - Tiny green check marks may appear under some cups, but keep it clean.
   - NO readable text.

3. Middle board, about 48 percent of screen:
   - A clear grid play area, 4 columns by 4 rows.
   - Some cells are empty beige rounded squares.
   - Some cells contain small cream trays with exactly six circular cup holes arranged 3 by 2.
   - Trays can be partially filled, not always full.
   - Show example gameplay state:
     - one tray has two green fruit tea cups,
     - one tray has three pink strawberry tea cups,
     - one tray has four milk tea cups,
     - one tray has mixed green and pink cups,
     - one tray is full with six identical pink cups and looks ready to deliver.
   - Adjacent trays with matching drinks should visually imply drinks can move/merge between trays, using subtle arrows or sparkle trails, but no text.
   - All cups should have consistent style but different liquid colors, toppings, cup stickers, and straw/cap details.

4. Bottom tray spawn area, about 16 percent of screen:
   - A warm tabletop strip.
   - Exactly three available trays are shown side by side.
   - Each tray has six cup holes and is partially filled with drinks.
   - The third slot can be empty placeholder if desired, but prefer three visible trays.
   - This area should clearly read as the "next trays to place" area.

5. Very bottom tool button area, about 6 percent of screen:
   - Three rounded orange tool buttons with simple icons only:
     remove one tray, reshuffle trays, clear one row.
   - Icons only or fake unreadable marks, NO readable text.

Strict constraints:
- NO English text, NO Chinese text, NO readable words, NO numbers, NO watermark, NO QR code.
- Keep all important gameplay objects inside safe margins.
- The image should look like a polished game prototype screenshot, not a marketing poster.
- Prioritize clean object readability over character detail.
