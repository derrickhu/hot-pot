# 每日限定上方卡牌区薄边绿色底框 v2

## 用途

每日限定玩法上方堆叠卡牌区专用底框。上一版圆角过重、边框太厚，本版要求更贴近参考游戏：薄边、直角感、轻量、不抢卡牌视觉。

## Gemini 提示词

```text
Single game UI asset: an empty top card-board frame for a cute 2D casual mobile fruit puzzle game.

Style:
- Polished 2D hand-painted mobile game UI asset.
- Fresh green fruit-garden style, but clean and lightweight.
- Similar feeling to a casual fruit card puzzle board.
- Soft cel shading, subtle highlights, clean outline.

Shape and proportions:
- One wide horizontal rectangular board frame, aspect ratio about 1.25:1 to 1.35:1.
- Corners must be almost square, only a very tiny radius. Do NOT make big rounded corners.
- The border must be thin and elegant, not chunky, not thick.
- Use a slim double-line frame: darker green outer stroke, lighter fresh green inner stroke.
- Interior is one clean flat pale mint / pale cream panel.
- Interior must be empty and simple.

Decoration:
- Very subtle grape leaves / vine curls only outside or on the outer edge corners.
- Small grape accents are allowed on the outer bottom corners, but keep them tiny.
- Decorations must not enter the interior card area.
- The frame should not cover too much area; most space should be usable interior.

Important constraints:
- NO card slots, NO grooves, NO grid, NO inset holes.
- NO cards, NO fruit icons inside the board.
- NO text, no labels, no captions, no writing anywhere.
- NO logo, no watermark, no buttons, no characters, no full scene background.
- Generate only one isolated board frame centered on a plain white or very pale background, easy to remove.
- Do not crop the frame; full rectangular frame visible.
```
